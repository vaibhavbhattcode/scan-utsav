# ScanUtsav — Design Token System & Visual Standards

## COLORS
- **Ink** (near-black base, not pure black): `#14100D`
- **Marigold** (primary accent, warmer/richer than flat orange): `#F2810C`
- **Deep Maroon** (secondary accent, wedding/festive richness): `#7A1E2B`
- **Gold Foil** (for premium/paid-plan accents, used sparingly): `#C9A24B`
- **Cream/Parchment** (light-mode surface + card backgrounds on dark): `#F5EDE0`
- **Emerald** (success & live-indicator, warm tone): `#1F8A6F`

## TYPOGRAPHY
- **Display Face**: High-contrast serif / display face with Indian celebration poster energy (`Fraunces` / `Canela`).
- **Body Face**: Humanist sans for high readability (`Inter`).
- **Mono Face**: Utility monospace for QR codes, event tokens, and timestamps.

## LAYOUT SIGNATURE
- **Signature Rangoli & Diya Motif**: Elegant thin border motifs used as section dividers.
- **Event-Type Dynamic Skins**:
  - `wedding`: Deep Maroon & Gold Foil
  - `ganesh-chaturthi`: Marigold & Crimson
  - `navratri`: Vibrant Purple & Royal Gold
  - `janmashtami`: Peacock Blue & Gold
  - `birthday`: Playful Bright Palette
  - `corporate`: Sleek Platinum Ink & Gold

## MOTION
- Orchestrated hero entrance sequence.
- Restrained scroll-triggered reveals (`framer-motion`).
- Global `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.
